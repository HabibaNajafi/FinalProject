Band-e-Amir National Park

Known for its stunning blue lakes, dramatic cliffs, and peaceful environment, Band-e-Amir is one of Afghanistan's most beautiful natural wonders.

  • Six breathtaking lakes
  • Perfect for nature lovers
  • Ideal for hiking and photography
  • Best time to visit: May–September
